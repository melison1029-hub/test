Questions

What people actually ask

What does it cost to sell with you?

Commission is negotiable and agreed in writing before your home goes on the market. You'll get a full cost-of-sale breakdown — commission, transfer fees, attorney and closing costs — with an estimated net-proceeds figure, so there are no surprises at the table.

How fast can you get my home on the market?

Typically about a week from signed agreement to live listing — that covers prep, photography, floor plans, copy and MLS entry. If you need faster, say so and we'll tell you honestly what we can compress.

Do I need to renovate before listing?

Usually not much. We'll give you a prioritized list of what returns more than it costs — typically paint, lighting, landscaping and decluttering — and tell you plainly which projects you won't get your money back on.

As a buyer, what do I pay you?

We sign a written buyer agreement stating exactly how we're compensated and by whom before touring a single home. Seller-paid compensation is negotiable on every deal, and we'll tell you where it stands on each property.

Can I buy and sell at the same time?

Yes, and it's a large part of what we do. We build one timeline covering both sides — contingency structure, bridge options, rent-back — so you're never forced into a bad offer or a rushed purchase.

How do I get into the clientele portal?

Once you sign with us, we send you a login. Sellers get a live tracker of their sale; buyers get their showing calendar and offer status. It updates as we work, so you're not waiting on a phone call to know where things stand.
