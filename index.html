07 — Questions

The things people
actually ask

What does it cost to sell with you?

Commission is negotiable and agreed in writing before your home goes on the market. I'll walk you through the full cost of sale — commission, transfer taxes, attorney and closing fees — and give you an estimated net-proceeds figure so there are no surprises at the table.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on price, condition and season. At our first meeting I'll show you current days-on-market for comparable homes in your area and model what each pricing scenario is likely to do to that timeline.

Should I renovate before listing?

Usually not much. I'll give you a prioritized punch list of what returns more than it costs — typically paint, lighting, landscaping and decluttering — and tell you plainly which projects you will not get your money back on.

Does a buyer pay me anything?

We'll sign a written buyer agreement that states exactly how I'm compensated and by whom before we tour a single property. Seller-paid compensation is negotiable on every transaction, and I'll tell you where it stands on each home we see.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Less than most people assume. Programs range from 3% conventional to 0% for qualifying VA buyers, plus down-payment assistance in many areas. I'll connect you with lenders who will map the real options against your numbers.

I'm buying and selling at the same time. Can that work?

Yes, and it's most of what I do. We build one timeline covering both sides — including contingency structure, bridge options and rent-back — so you're never forced to accept a bad offer or make a rushed purchase.
